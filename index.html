Caselist Mailer is a Chrome extension for policy debaters. It finds the speech doc you emailed, fills in the round from your Tabroom pairing, and publishes it to openCaselist. It also looks up what the other team has disclosed.

What Caselist Mailer does

Disclosure is the chore you skip because it means retyping what's already in your email. Both sections start from what you already have.

Publish

Turns the doc you emailed into a published round.

  1. Open the panel. It reads your recent round mail and suggests the round you owe, with your speech doc already chosen.
  2. Check the doc and the round. Tournament, side, round, opponent and judge come from your Tabroom pairing; if there is no pairing yet, it reads the chain's subject line instead. Change the doc from the list under the card, or any field behind Check details.
  3. Press Publish. Nothing is uploaded until you do. The doc goes to openCaselist as open source, with a round report if you wrote one and a link to the recording if you have one. No speech doc to hand? Upload a file instead — it reads the round off the filename and fills in what it can.

Opponents

Shows you what the other team has disclosed, without opening the site.

  1. Don't type anything. It reads your latest pairing and puts the team you're about to debate on the card, on the side they'll be on: the aff they're most likely to read, and the 2NRs they go for most often.
  2. Or type any team. School › Team › Tournament › Round: press any part of the trail to see the others at that level, and open the round you want.
  3. Take the doc. Each round that has one carries a download, and the 1AC carries a view button straight to openCaselist.

Install

About three minutes. Two of the steps look scarier than they are, so they're explained.

  1. Add it to Chrome

    From the Chrome Web Store. Pin it to the toolbar so it's one click away.

  2. Open the side panel

    Click the icon. The panel opens beside whatever page you're on, and stays open while you switch tabs.

  3. Sign in with Google

    You'll see a screen that says Google hasn't verified this app. Caselist isn't verified yet, so Google shows that warning. Click Advanced, then Go to Caselist Mailer, then Continue.

    It asks for one permission: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It uses it only to find the speech docs you and your partner emailed, the Tabroom pairing email for the round, and the file-share emails Tabroom sends. It can't send, change or delete mail.

  4. Be signed in to openCaselist

    Sign in at opencaselist.com once, in this browser. No tab has to stay open: Caselist talks to openCaselist directly, using the sign-in your browser already has, so it never sees a password or a token.

  5. Set up once

    Choose your caselist, school and team. It offers your partner's address from the mail you have already exchanged — press Use, or type it yourself. That's it; it remembers.

What it can see

  • Your Gmail, read-only. It opens three kinds of message: round emails sent by you or your partner, the Tabroom pairing emails that name your round, and the file-share emails Tabroom sends for a round's share link. Messages from anyone else on a chain are never read; the extension looks at who was on the thread, nothing more.
  • openCaselist, signed in as you. Caselist talks to openCaselist directly, using the sign-in your browser already has. No tab has to be open, and it never sees or stores a password or a token.
  • The only part of your mail that leaves your browser is the round you choose to publish. There's no server. Feedback goes to the developer only if you type it and press Send. Your setup, your partner's address and the team index it builds all live in the extension's local storage on your machine.
