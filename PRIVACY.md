# Caselist Mailer Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 9, 2026**

Short version: [What Caselist can and can't access](ACCESS.html)

Caselist is a Chrome extension that helps debaters publish their speech documents to openCaselist and scout opponents. It has no server of its own: everything it does happens in your browser. There is no account to create, and no analytics or tracking.

## What Caselist reads from Gmail

If you connect Gmail, Caselist uses Google's read-only Gmail permission. It can't send, delete or change your mail. It reads:

- your Gmail address;
- your recent round emails (email chains with speech documents attached, Tabroom pairing emails, and Tabroom file-share emails), including their subjects, dates, senders and recipients, and attachment names;
- the attachment you choose to publish, when you press Publish.

It reads these when you open the side panel, when you search your mail, and when you open Settings (to suggest your partner from the names and addresses on your round emails; it doesn't read message text for this).

While the side panel is open, it checks about once a minute whether new round emails have arrived, using the same searches; it doesn't read other new mail.

You can use Caselist without connecting Gmail by uploading files instead.

## What Caselist reads from openCaselist

Caselist talks to openCaselist directly, using the openCaselist sign-in already in your browser. It reads the caselists, schools, teams and rounds you can see there (including debaters' names as openCaselist lists them), and the Tabroom rounds linked to your openCaselist account.

## What leaves your browser

- To openCaselist, only when you press Publish: the round you publish (tournament, round, side, opponent, judge, any round report or recording link you add) and its file.
- To the developer, only if you type feedback and press Send: your message and the extension's version number, through a Google Form. Nothing else is attached. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Forms.

Your mail is never sent to the developer or to any other party.

## What Caselist stores on your computer

In your browser's extension storage (never on a server):

- your settings: caselist, school, team, partner addresses, how far back to look, tournament numbering, whether you use Caselist without Gmail, and which sections are open;
- your Gmail address;
- a record of up to 200 rounds you've published (Gmail message and attachment IDs, the team, and the date);
- the IDs of up to 2,000 emails already checked and found not to be round emails, so they aren't read again;
- a list of openCaselist schools and teams, with debaters' names as openCaselist lists them.

The contents of your emails are never saved. While the panel is open, recently read emails are kept in memory for up to five minutes, then discarded.

## How your data is used

Data from Google is used only to provide Caselist's features: finding your rounds, filling in the publish form, and showing your next opponent. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. No person reads your data, except feedback you choose to send.

Caselist's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

- Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.
- To revoke Gmail access, go to myaccount.google.com/connections and remove Caselist Mailer.
- Rounds you published live on openCaselist; delete them there.

## Children

Caselist is meant for high school and college debaters and is not directed at children under 13.
